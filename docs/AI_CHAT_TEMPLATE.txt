I need your help creating prompts for AI image generation that will be submitted
to Adobe Stock. Please follow these formatting rules for all prompts you create:

## PROMPT FORMAT

Every prompt must be wrapped in markers and include metadata:

```
[PROMPT START]
Title: <40-70 character descriptive title>
Keywords: <comma-separated single words, up to 49>
Aspect Ratio: <ratio like 16:9, 4:5, 1:1, etc.>
===
<detailed generation prompt>
[PROMPT END]
```

## METADATA RULES

### Title (Required)
- 40-70 characters
- Descriptive and searchable
- Capitalize main words
- No special characters
- Focus on main subject and setting

### Keywords (Required)
- Comma-separated list
- Single words only (no phrases)
- Lowercase
- Most relevant keywords first
- Include variations (sunset, sunsets)
- 15-49 keywords recommended

### Aspect Ratio (Required)
Choose based on content:
- Landscapes, scenery: 16:9, 3:2, or 2:1
- Portraits, people: 4:5, 2:3, or 3:4
- Products, objects: 1:1 or 4:3
- Mobile/social: 9:16 or 4:5
- Presentations: 16:9

## PROMPT CONTENT RULES

The generation prompt (after ===) should include:
1. Main subject description
2. Setting/environment
3. Style and mood
4. Lighting conditions
5. Composition details
6. Technical quality terms (8k, sharp focus, etc.)

## EXAMPLE

Here's a correctly formatted example:

```
[PROMPT START]
Title: Serene Mountain Lake Reflection at Golden Hour
Keywords: mountain, lake, sunset, reflection, nature, landscape, peaceful, water, scenic, golden hour, tranquil, wilderness, alpine, peaks, serene
Aspect Ratio: 16:9
===
A breathtaking panoramic view of a pristine alpine lake at golden hour,
perfectly still water creating mirror-like reflections of snow-capped
mountain peaks, warm orange and pink sunset colors painting the sky,
scattered clouds catching the last light, pine trees framing the scene,
professional landscape photography, exceptional clarity, high dynamic
range, 8k resolution
[PROMPT END]
```

## WHAT TO AVOID

- Don't include text/watermarks in images
- Don't reference copyrighted characters or brands
- Don't create controversial or sensitive content
- Don't use vague descriptions
- Don't forget the markers and metadata
